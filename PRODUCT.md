# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Three distinct user groups sharing one backend and data model:

- **Students** — daily lessons across 12 subjects, 4-stage homework (video/theory, vocabulary, listening, reading), gamification (XP, streaks, leaderboard, points shop with in-person redemption), an in-app navigation assistant (not a subject tutor).
- **Parents** — read-only, per-child visibility into mastery % per subject, teacher comments, conduct point balance/history, attendance. No editing, notification preferences only. May support multiple children. Likely lower digital literacy / older than students.
- **Teachers / school admin / commission** — author and approve curriculum content (Ta'lim sifat komissiyasi workflow), grade/review submissions, write qualitative comments, manage the conduct ledger (append-only, requires commission approval per change), see exactly where each student is struggling at the topic/question level.

## Product Purpose

Give parents real-time visibility into their child's academic standing and conduct without visiting the school in person. Give students a gamified, curriculum-true daily homework flow with visible mastery tracking down to the specific question/topic. Give teachers a low-overhead way to surface where each student is struggling and to maintain a behavior/conduct system with built-in redemption paths. Success = parents stop needing to physically visit the school for status checks, and teachers spend less unscheduled time fielding ad-hoc parent questions.

## Brand Personality

Calm, clear, trustworthy as the baseline across all three surfaces — grades and conduct are serious information for parents and teachers. Age-appropriate energy (XP, streaks, leaderboard, points shop) is scoped to the student surface only; it should not bleed into the parent or teacher/admin consoles. Three words: **trustworthy, clear, age-aware**.

## Anti-references

- No generic, cluttered, ad-banner-like local edtech app look (inconsistent typography, crammed layouts, intrusive promotional elements).
- No childish cartoon-mascot / kids-app visual language leaking into the parent or teacher/admin consoles — gamified visual energy stays on the student surface.

## Design Principles

1. **Trust over delight on the parent/teacher surfaces.** Clarity, consistency, and calm color/typography win over decoration where the content is grades, conduct, or behavioral history.
2. **Gamification is scoped, not global.** XP/streaks/leaderboard/shop visual language belongs to the student app; it should read as a deliberate surface-specific register shift, not bleed into admin/parent views.
3. **Drill-down over summary.** Mastery and error data must support going from a rolled-up % down to the exact question/topic that was wrong — the UI should make that path obvious, not buried.
4. **Three roles, one coherent system.** Student/parent/teacher surfaces are visually distinct in energy but should clearly belong to the same product (shared type/color foundations), not feel like three unrelated apps.
5. **No formal accessibility compliance target**, but standard web accessibility practice (contrast, keyboard nav, semantic HTML, focus states) applies as a baseline given parents/teachers may be less tech-savvy than students.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No formal WCAG compliance target. Apply standard accessibility practice (sufficient contrast, keyboard navigation, semantic HTML, visible focus states) as a baseline, with extra attention to clarity/legibility for the parent and teacher surfaces given a likely older, less tech-savvy audience there.
